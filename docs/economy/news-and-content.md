Stay informed with articles, updates, and live content from the Bobina Council.

### News Articles

The [News](https://bobina.moe/news) section features community-written articles about the Bobina Council ecosystem, crypto insights, and platform developments. Articles can be upvoted by the community and are written by council members and moderators.

- Rich text editor with formatting, images, and embedded content
- Community voting system to highlight popular articles
- View counts and engagement metrics
- Tag-based organization for easy discovery

### Changelog

The [Changelog](https://bobina.moe/changelog) keeps you updated on all platform improvements, new features, and bug fixes. Each update is documented with detailed descriptions and timestamps.

- Chronological list of all platform updates
- Detailed descriptions of changes and improvements
- Automatic notifications on X, Telegram or Discord for major updates
- Version tracking and release notes

### Streams

The [Streams](https://bobina.moe/streams) section features live streams and recorded content from the Bobina community, including AMAs, trading sessions, and community events.

- Embedded video player for seamless viewing
- Community voting on stream content
- Scheduled live streams and archived recordings
- Integration with popular streaming platforms

All content types support community engagement through voting, comments, and sharing, ensuring the most valuable content rises to the top.
